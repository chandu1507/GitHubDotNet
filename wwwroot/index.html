How to play
  • Slide (left/right keys): continuous movement. Hold to accelerate; release and the rod stops exactly there.
  • Tilt (up/down keys): tilts the feet toward the top/bottom of the screen. The angle stays where you leave it.
    • Upright: figures block the ball.
    • Tilted far back (feet faded): the ball rolls underneath. Lift your front rods so your own shots get through.
    • Tilted slightly forward (yellow foot outline): traps a slow ball. Slide to carry it, then kick for a pin shot (+25% power). There's a 10-second pin limit.
  • Kick: a fast forward swing. Hit the ball off-center to angle the shot, and slide while kicking to add spin. Kicking from a tilted-back angle is a wind-up and adds 20% power.
  • Long rallies speed the ball up. A stuck ball is re-served after 3 seconds.
  • Pressing Shift 5 times opens Windows Sticky Keys, so no Shift key is used for kicking.
